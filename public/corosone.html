      Blocked IP Addresses

      A blocked address can still open the website, but it cannot send enquiries or call requests, or sign in to the staff inbox. It is shown a message with your phone number and email instead, in case it is a real person on a shared network. This console is never blocked. The "Turned away" count updates about once a minute. Blocking a long address (one with colons) blocks the whole home or phone network it belongs to, shown ending in /64, because devices change their long address every day or so.

      Follow-up Alarm

      Enquiries nobody has acted on yet that arrived more than a day ago, oldest first. An enquiry counts as answered once staff move it to another stage or mark it handled.

      ReceivedNameContactWaiting

      Duplicate Finder

      Enquiries that look like the same person writing more than once: same email, phone, name and city, or device. The first one in each group is the original. Marking another as a duplicate moves it to Handled with a note; nothing is deleted.

      Holiday Switch

      Pause call bookings while you are away. The booking popup says when you are back and suggests that day, and the contact page shows a short note during the break, in every language. Messages through the contact form still arrive as usual.

      Dates are California dates, and both days are included. A break can be up to 120 days.

      IP Address Check

      Everything about one address: who owns its network (from the public internet registry), whether it looks like a VPN, Tor or a data centre, and everything it did on this site. The VPN check is a strong guess, never proof, and nothing is blocked automatically.

      Care-Home Licence Lookup

      Search California's list of licensed care homes for the elderly (RCFEs) by name, licensee, city, ZIP code or licence number. Details shows the state's inspection visits, complaints and citations. Data comes straight from the State of California.
